# Conciseness

2026-06-26

---

I've always struggled to write a lot of text; hitting the mandatory 1000-word minimum in essays was always difficult in high school.

Previously, I felt that writing a short essay meant that I didn't have enough content. Now, in the age of genAI (and thus, an abundance of text), I've realized that conciseness is appreciated and often preferred.

Write concisely.
